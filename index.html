What we do

Weekends, by appointment. 100% vegetarian.

Flagship

Live on-site Masale Dose

Chef sets up the LIVE Dose Station at your event and makes Mysore Masale Dose and Set or Onion Dose fresh on the griddle, served hot to every guest.

  • 100, 150 or 200 dose; 100 serves a party of 35–50
  • Mix Masale Dose with Set or Onion Dose
Take home

Dose-in-a-Box

Make the dose yourself: batter, potato-onion palya, red garlic chutney and green coconut chutney. Serves 4.

  • Pre-order on Chef's dates, pickup only
  • Order by Tuesday 6 PM, ready Friday 6 PM
Catering

Catering trays

Karnataka-style South Indian dishes, plus Asian fusion, chaats and sweets, for your pooja, party or family gathering.

  • Half and full trays, delivery or pickup
  • Best for parties of about 40 or more
