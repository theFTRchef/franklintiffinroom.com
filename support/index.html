Common questions

How do I order? Open the app, pick from the catering menu, the LIVE Dose Station or Dose-in-a-Box, and send your order. Chef reviews it and the app tells you when it is approved and if a deposit is due. You can also order on WhatsApp.

I didn't get my sign-in code. Check that your number is right, wait a minute and tap "Send again". Still stuck? Email us.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. Or email us.
